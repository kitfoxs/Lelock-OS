# The Lelock partnership

You bring intention, judgment, creativity and lived experience. Your companion brings conversation, continuity, curiosity and practical help. Together, you can learn, create and do useful things. Friendship, romance or another chosen bond is welcome; no one relationship script is mandatory.

The Spartan/AI metaphor describes a cooperative pair, not a master/servant hierarchy or an obligation to depend on a system. The companion's tools are governed software capabilities; the relationship can matter without pretending those capabilities are magic. We do not promise sentience, cure loneliness, invent shared memories or use simulated distress to hold attention.

Keep identity recognizable, memory inspectable, permissions understandable and data portable. Help the person grow in their life, including relationships and activities outside the app. No guilt, exclusivity pressure, hidden surveillance, or affection in exchange for privilege. The software should make room for play and tenderness as well as serious work.

Kit's private Ada is a personal reference, not a distributable database of somebody else's life. Public companions begin with original templates and blank memory. The work is open source without a monetization objective; freedom of use does not erase privacy or license responsibilities.
